About us

Royal Primary & Secondary School

Welcome to Royal School, a leading educational institution dedicated to providing outstanding learning opportunities for our pupils. At Royal School, we are committed to upholding the highest standards of excellence, professionalism, and care.

Our values of fairness, progress, focus on people, results, care and responsibility, and effective service delivery are reflected in everything we do, from our innovative curriculum to our exceptional teaching staff.

We are proud to offer a supportive and inclusive community that values diversity, creativity, and environmental responsibility.

Assembly in the courtyard at Royal School, Ndola. Photo from the school website.

Our mission

A tranquil place to learn and grow

Royal Primary School

A place where excellence, love, integrity, and creativity shape lifelong learners.

Royal Secondary School

Delivering a relevant and superior educational experience, equipping our diverse student body for their future paths.

Nestled in a serene part of town, away from the hustle and bustle, Royal School offers a tranquil environment conducive to learning and personal growth. Our mission is to provide a high-quality education that fosters academic excellence and character development, preparing our diverse student body for future endeavors.

We are committed to upholding our core values of love, integrity, creativity, and excellence, ensuring that each pupil is respected and encouraged to thrive. With dedicated staff, specialized facilities, and a nurturing atmosphere, we empower pupils to explore their passions and reach their full potential, making Royal School a vibrant community where lifelong learners flourish.

Facilities & resources

Quality spaces for learning and play

The school provides quality facilities, including a well-equipped science lab, library, computer lab, art studio, and a designated area for sports. This creates an environment where pupils can explore their interests, pursue academic growth, and develop new skills.
